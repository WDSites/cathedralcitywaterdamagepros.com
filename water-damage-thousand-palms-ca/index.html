How runoff and mud reach Thousand Palms

Thousand Palms is a census-designated place, not a city, with a 2020 population of 7,967 and an elevation of about 246 feet. Desert storm water does not soak in gently. It sheets across hard ground, gathers in low channels and carries sand and silt with it, so the damage here is often mud first and moisture second.

That order matters for the clean-up. Mud tracked across a floor is not clean water, and it dries into a cement-like film in the cracks of tile and under baseboards. Our flood damage restoration work starts by treating the silt as contaminated debris.

What October 11, 2026 showed on Varner Road

During the October 11, 2026 storm remnants, Varner Road was temporarily closed between Edom Hill Road and Mountain View Road in the Willow Hole area so crews could remove mud. If your route runs through that stretch, check current road notices before you leave and plan a different way around. Do not test a muddy road with your car.

A vehicle that sits in flowing or standing water can stall. Park on higher ground and keep the garage clear if you know a storm is coming. Call after the storm for emergency water removal if the garage has taken water.

Ramon Road at Date Palm Drive: the crossing to watch

On the same day, Ramon Road at Date Palm Drive had standing water. Those two roads are the usual routes between Cathedral City and Thousand Palms, so a closed or flooded crossing changes how crews reach you and how you reach work.

Turn around, don't drown. Choose a different road, and tell the dispatcher which streets you used so we can plan our own route. If water is inside your home, the delay is worth it, because a crew that arrives safely can start pumping.

Mud inside garages and at doors

Garages take the first hit because the door seal sits flush with the slab. Silt pushes in under the door, wets cardboard boxes and drywall at the base, then runs to the house door. Pull boxes off the floor, sweep the heavy mud out with a flat shovel, and leave the finer film for the crew.

We lift the wet material, run extraction units across the slab and set air movers and an LGR dehumidifier along the shared wall. A moisture meter tells us whether the wall base needs a flood cut. That is water damage cleanup for the debris and sanitizing, done in step with the drying.

If water is still spreading in Thousand Palms

Stop what you can

If the water comes from a broken pipe, close the main valve. If it comes from outside, move to dry ground and wait for it to stop before sweeping.

Power and water do not mix

If electrical lines or the meter sit in water, do not touch them. Call your electric provider and 911 first. Cut the panel only when it is dry.

Get items off the floor

Raise documents, electronics and anything made of cardboard. Pull rugs and wet bedding out of mud contact if you can do it safely.

Keep family out of mud

Silt and runoff are dirty water. Keep children and pets back, wear rubber boots and gloves, and wash after contact.

Phone the 24/7 line

Describe how deep the water is and which rooms are wet. Take photos, then let us know.

Power, meters and Imperial Irrigation District

Imperial Irrigation District is the electricity provider for Thousand Palms. After a flood, if power lines are down or the meter is sitting in water, stay away and call your electric provider. Restoration comes after the utility has made the area safe. Never wade toward a submerged panel.

Preserve edges, arena traffic and nearby hills

The Coachella Valley Preserve covers 20,000 acres of desert oasis, dunes and fan palm groves. Properties near its edge catch fine sand and dust that tracks in on boots and wet tires. Mats at doors and a sweep before the mop keep that grit from grinding into flooring.

Acrisure Arena sits on Varner Road, which means event traffic can crowd the same roads our trucks need. Allow extra planning time on show nights. If you live in the Indio Hills area, the hills area nearby, tell us your approach road when you call.

Repairs, permits and who handles your address

Because Thousand Palms is unincorporated, permits for repairs may go through the county rather than a city. Ask the county or your local building authority which office handles your address. Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Thousand Palms?

Yes. We serve 92276, the whole of the community, including homes along Ramon Road, Date Palm Drive and Varner Road. If you are unsure about your address, give the dispatcher the cross streets.

Is emergency water removal available in Thousand Palms?

Yes. The line is open 24/7 and same-day response is available when crews are free. Road closures after a storm can slow everyone down, so we ask for your safest access route.

Should I sweep mud out of my garage myself?

You can remove the heavy mud with a shovel and gloves. Leave the thin film, wet drywall and cardboard to a crew, since silt is contaminated and the wall base may need a moisture check.

Will my homeowner policy cover flood damage?

Check your policy. Mud and runoff from a wash are flood losses, and flood damage is usually excluded from a standard policy and needs its own coverage. Take photos and keep a log of calls.
