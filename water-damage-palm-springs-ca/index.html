If your house is from that run, its original water lines have had decades of use, so ask a plumber to check them and tell dispatch the build year when you call.

We start with a moisture meter and thermal imaging, because stains on the surface rarely show how far the wet area really runs.

What older construction changes about a leak

General trade knowledge applies here. Older supply lines corrode from the inside, and a pinhole can weep into a wall cavity for weeks before a stain appears. Water heaters from the same era of remodels often sit in closets or garages, where a failed tank soaks flooring before anyone hears it.

Flat and low-slope roofs, common on mid-century designs, drain slowly and can pond water at seams and penetrations. Plaster, lath and early drywall also behave differently from newer board. Plaster holds moisture longer, and older insulation can wick water upward. We map moisture on every surface before deciding what stays and what gets cut out.

  • Slab-on-grade floors hide supply lines in or under the concrete, so a hot spot on tile can mean a leak below.
  • Old galvanized or thin copper lines fail at fittings first, usually inside a wall.
  • Flat roof ponding shows up as ceiling stains below parapets and roof drains.
  • Painted-over stains often mean a leak that has been active more than once.

If water is still spreading in Palm Springs

Find the valve

Locate the main shutoff before you need it, usually at the house or a meter box near the property line. Turn it clockwise until it stops, and open one faucet to drain the lines.

Check the panel first

If water reached outlets, the panel or a ceiling fixture, stay out. If the floor near the breaker box is wet, stay away from it and phone the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk before you call us.

Save what you can carry

Move rugs, books, mid-century furniture legs and electronics onto dry surfaces. Foil or plastic under wood furniture feet stops stain transfer on wet floors.

Stay out of dirty water

Water that came in from the yard, a drain or a toilet is not clean. Keep children and pets away from it, and leave gray or black water to the crew.

Rain history that still matters to your walls

Palm Springs averages about 4.93 inches of rain a year, but the record shows how far that can swing. The wettest rain year, July 1926 to June 1927, delivered 17.68 inches, including 10.22 inches in three days in mid-February. Flooding in 1893 damaged crops and irrigation systems. Soil and roofs here are built for drought, so a heavy storm finds every weak seam.

On October 11, 2026, city-shared video showed water near the Biltmore around 9 p.m., and Vista Chino at the wash closed in coordination with Cathedral City. If water crossed your threshold that night, document it, photograph the waterline and call early. Wet drywall and padding that sit for days become much harder to dry in place.

Which service fits your Palm Springs problem

Pooled water on a floor calls for emergency water removal with pumps and extraction units. Once the house is dry, water damage repair replaces drywall, insulation, baseboard and flooring, and we match finishes to what your home already has. Ask any crew whether they work to ANSI/IICRC S500, the water damage standard.

Insurance questions come up on most calls. Policies tend to treat a burst pipe, a slow leak and a flood as three different things, so check yours before you assume. Keep a log of calls and reports, and make temporary repairs to protect the property. Dry logs and moisture readings give your insurer clear documentation.

Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Palm Springs?

Yes. We serve 92262 and 92264, from the Palm Canyon Drive corridor and the Movie Colony and Vista Las Palmas areas through the Racquet Club Estates and Deepwell Estates neighborhoods. If your street is not obvious, call and give us the address.

Is emergency water removal available in Palm Springs?

Yes. Our 24/7 emergency line is open nights, weekends and holidays, and we offer same-day response when available. No arrival time is promised, though you will be told what to do while the crew travels.

Can older Palm Springs homes be dried without tearing out walls?

Sometimes. Drilling small holes and using injected air can dry cavities in some cases, but plaster, wet insulation or contaminated water may require removal. Moisture readings decide, and we explain scope and price before work starts.

Does a mid-century roof leak need a different repair?

It can. Low-slope and flat roofs need the roof fixed first, then ceilings dried and repaired. A roofer handles the membrane, and we handle the interior drying and rebuild.
