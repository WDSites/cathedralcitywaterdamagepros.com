Five Cove communities, one city, many kinds of homes

Rancho Mirage was incorporated on August 3, 1973 from five Cove communities: Rancho Mirage, Desert, Palmas, Tamarisk and Thunderbird. That history explains why a single zip code holds so many different homes, from older clubside houses to newer estates, each with its own plumbing age. Tell us which community you live in, and we plan around the likely pipe and roof type.

Country clubs, resorts and the association rules behind them

Thunderbird opened in 1950, Tamarisk in the 1950s, Mission Hills in 1979 and Rancho Mirage Country Club in 1985. The Westin Mission Hills Resort and Spa followed in 1987, and Rancho Las Palmas, now Omni, opened in 1979. Most of these sit inside gated communities with HOAs, so a leak in your unit can reach a neighbor, and the association may have rules about who can enter, when and how.

Before any crew arrives, check your association's rules on contractor access, gate lists and water shutoff. Large homes in these communities often have long plumbing runs, several bathrooms and more than one water heater, so a single failed line can wet rooms far from the source. Irrigation and pool equipment leaks are common trouble spots on golf-course lots.

  • Tell the gate or management office a restoration crew is coming.
  • Ask the HOA who owns the wet wall in a condo or villa.
  • Shut off the irrigation controller if the leak started outside.
  • Photograph pool equipment pads, since slow leaks there can undermine slabs.

Restaurant Row, the casino and the hospital

Highway 111 runs through Rancho Mirage, and the stretch between Bob Hope Drive and Frank Sinatra Drive is known as Restaurant Row. Kitchens, grease lines and dishwashers make those buildings prone to drain backups that need quick containment. Agua Caliente Casino sits at Bob Hope Drive and Ramon Road, and Eisenhower Medical Center is the largest employer in town. For any commercial or institutional leak, a 24/7 line matters because the doors cannot close.

When water is spreading in Rancho Mirage

A broken supply line in a large house can run unnoticed through a guest wing for hours. Our emergency dispatch is available around the clock, and we offer same-day response when available. We pump out standing water, pull wet baseboard and cut away saturated drywall, then set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. That sequence is the heart of water mitigation and keeps small losses from becoming mold losses.

When the surfaces are dry, water damage repair puts back drywall, insulation, paint and flooring. If moisture sat long enough to grow mold, mold remediation follows S520 practice, with containment and HEPA air filtration. Ask any company whether its crews work to ANSI/IICRC S500 and S520.

If water is still spreading in Rancho Mirage

Close the main, then the heater

Large homes may have more than one shutoff and more than one water heater. Close the main first, then each heater's inlet valve if you can find it.

Power safety

If water is near outlets, light fixtures or the panel, stay away. Flip breakers only from a dry spot, and ring the utility or 911 about electrical or gas danger before you call our line.

Lift what stains

Move rugs, art, upholstered pieces and electronics out of the wet area. Stone and hardwood floors need the surface water gone quickly.

Keep people clear

Water from a toilet, floor drain or outdoor source is contaminated. Keep family, guests and pets out until a crew tests and clears the area.

Call, then notify the HOA

Dial our 24/7 line, then tell your association and management. Note the time you found the water for your claim file.

The 1979 flood and your flood map

A flood in 1979 along the Magnesia Spring Creek alluvial fan killed one person and caused $7 million in damage. The Army Corps of Engineers later installed concrete-lined spillways. Those works do not remove your own risk, so check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurer about separate flood coverage. Standard homeowner policies usually exclude flood. For rising water, see our flood damage restoration work.

Insurance, paperwork and neighboring cities

A burst irrigation or supply line is usually judged differently from a long-term drip. Report the loss, keep a log of calls and make temporary repairs. Frequently asked questions

Do you cover every ZIP code in Rancho Mirage?

Yes. We serve 92270, which runs from Highway 111 and Restaurant Row through Bob Hope Drive, Frank Sinatra Drive and Ramon Road. That includes the gated communities, the clubs and the resorts along those corridors.

Is emergency water removal available in Rancho Mirage?

Yes. Our emergency line runs 24/7, including nights and holidays, with same-day response when available. We cannot promise an arrival time, but we will walk you through the first steps while the crew heads to you.

Do I need HOA approval before a restoration crew starts?

Often you need to notify the association, and some communities require gate access or contractor registration. Call management early. Emergency extraction usually cannot wait, but the HOA can tell you who handles shared walls and pipes.

Can irrigation or pool equipment leaks damage the house?

Yes. Slow leaks can saturate soil beside a foundation, wick into stucco and trim, and show up as interior stains. A moisture survey can tell whether the water came from outside or from plumbing.
